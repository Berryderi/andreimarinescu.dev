01Problem

AI agents repeat confident wrong facts. I wanted a reference they load first, where every claim has a checked source.

02What I built

  • Topic notes from zero to working competence.
  • Sources that are free to access, tiered by credibility, and each entry says why that source over the alternatives.
  • A CORRECTIONS file per area for widely repeated claims that turned out wrong.
  • Short topic guides built from it for quick reference.

03Results

29 topic trees · 1,195 notes.

04What I learned

Corrections are the most valuable part: the popular number is often wrong.
