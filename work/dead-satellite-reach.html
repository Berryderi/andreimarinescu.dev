01Problem

An idea for a station that clears dead satellites starts with a simple question: which ones could it even get to?

02What I built

An interactive estimate with sliders for altitude, orbit plane, fuel per trip and waiting time.

03How it works

  • CelesTrak catalog data, filtered to non-operational, near-circular payloads tilted 90 to 105 degrees between 400 and 2,000 km.
  • A one-way plane and altitude change, scanned every 5 days through the wait, with orbit planes drifting at constant J2 rates.
  • Tests compare against counts from a separate Python calculation on the same data.

04Results

What it leaves out

  • The return trip.
  • Phasing inside the orbit.
  • Catching tumbling objects.
  • Long-term inclination drift.
  • Whether each satellite is really dead.

Not reviewed by a spacecraft engineer.

05What I learned

Say what the model leaves out, on the page itself. The station doesn't exist and no owner has agreed to anything; the page says so.
