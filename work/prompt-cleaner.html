Prompt Cleaner

Select text, press a hotkey, and filler words and typos are cleaned in place. Text never leaves my PC.

01Problem

I dictate most of my prompts. Dictation leaves "um", "like" and typos, and a model asked to clean text tends to rewrite it.

02What I built

Rules first, then a local model, then a guard that removes any word the model added and restores content it dropped. Only filler, function words and repeats may disappear. In a terminal the hotkey opens a small dictation box instead.

03How it was tested

A test set of 9 of my own real prompts; 96 unit tests.

04Results

  • 9/9 on my test set with no content lost (after v1.1).
  • About 2 seconds up to 100 words.
  • Zero installs.

The failure: v1 dropped real content on 2 of the 9 samples and the similarity check missed it. v1.1 added the repair guard.

05What I learned

Never let a model edit your words without a guard that proves nothing new was added.
